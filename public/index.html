How to play

Every morning there's a new basket of groceries from DMart Ready. Guess what the whole basket costs, in rupees.

You get four guesses. After each one you get a nudge, never the answer:

  • Much higher / lower more than 20% off
  • Higher / Lower within 20%
  • A little higher / lower within 10%
  • Very close within 3%, but we won't say which way

Hit the total to the rupee for a bullseye. Otherwise your score is how close your best guess got.

A new shop appears every day at around 6 AM IST.
